# Season planning and troubleshooting

This page is **strategic advice**, except where it explicitly identifies an enforced rule. It is a way to organize decisions, not a guaranteed exploit or optimal tactic.

## Plan in three horizons

| Horizon | Primary question |
| --- | --- |
| Next match | Do we have eligible, fit players for the manager's positions? |
| Next window | Which contracts, loans and recruitment decisions must be settled? |
| Next season | Can finances, age cohorts and development support a stronger squad? |

A signing can be correct for one horizon and wrong for another. An expensive short-term fix may block a prospect and consume next season's wages; a future transfer may be excellent value but useless for today's shortage.

## Pre-season and the summer window

Start with the existing squad. Audit position coverage, injury/registration status, expiring contracts, wage hierarchy and prospects aging into new cohorts.

Separate needs into essential cover, genuine first-team improvement and optional opportunities. Retain alternatives for essential positions so a single failed negotiation does not dictate your entire window.

Set development plans and mentors before the season becomes busy. List players you are genuinely willing to loan, then compare incoming offers for pathway quality, not just fee or borrower reputation.

Consider infrastructure only after reserving the wages and cash required for the squad plan. The transfer budget is not the same as a rainy-day cash reserve.

## A weekly director routine

1. Read Inbox and resolve deadlines, incoming offers and events.
2. Review injuries, fitness, burnout, internationals and positional gaps.
3. Check active negotiations and scouting; act at the correct stage.
4. Review youth priorities, call-up proposals and loan performance.
5. Inspect wage room, cash movement and upcoming commitments.
6. Advance an appropriate interval, then inspect new outcomes.

Use the assistant's reports to shorten the audit, not replace it. Draft renewals are not signed renewals, a concern report is not a recall and a shortlist addition is not a scouting conclusion.

## Deadline week

Use one-day advances. Check outstanding personal terms, medical stages, rival/best-and-final rounds and scheduled starts.

Do not count a player until the agreement is completed and his intended registration is usable. If an essential signing slips, favor a lawful, affordable fallback over an unregistrable panic buy.

Review departures against remaining cover before accepting. A lucrative sale that leaves no eligible goalkeeper or no player for a formation slot can be more expensive than it looks.

## Winter review

Distinguish a structural problem from bad short-term results. Compare several games, expected chances, individual involvement and actual availability.

Check loans against promised minutes, investigate recurring fitness/burnout problems and renew players whose contracts are becoming urgent. The pre-contract window is an opportunity for permitted moves, not a universal right to approach any expiring player.

Use the second window to repair and refine rather than rebuild everything without evidence.

## Before rollover

Review U17/U19 age-outs, returning loans, purchase obligations, scheduled arrivals, expired contracts and instalments. Reserve room for obligations even when their future bill has been easy to ignore.

Promotion or relegation changes the context: revenue, budget, wage clauses, expectations, competition entry and the reserve team's legal level can shift. Do not simply copy this season's spending assumptions into the next.

Export a checkpoint you want to keep permanently; rotating snapshots are not an infinite archive.

## Common traps

### "He signed, but he cannot play"

**Rule:** signing, association registration, competition registration and match eligibility are separate. Read the dossier's specific reason, then check injury, suspension, age cohort, duty and squad assignment. [Registration guide](registration.md).

### "The player is fit, but the coach will not call him up"

**Rule:** Squad classifies fitness 60+ as fit, but coach call-ups require 70+. Merit, squad capacity, age, debut budget and match context can still prevent an opportunity. A development request does not force selection. [Development guide](development.md).

### "I cannot find a club to send my prospect to"

Use **Loans > Loan list** and respond to club-initiated loan offers. The affiliate search is not a general loan-out destination finder. List the player early, review offer frequency and wait for appropriate interest rather than inventing a missing destination workflow. [Loans guide](loans.md).

### "I can afford the fee, so why is the deal or budget blocked?"

Check cash, transfer allocation, wage room, future commitments, embargoes and applicable financial-regulation headroom separately. Instalments move timing; they do not erase cost. [Finance guide](club.md).

### "The prospect has stopped improving"

Compare age, potential gap, minutes, training continuity, staff/environment, injury and wellbeing. Potential is dynamic. Position training takes weeks; repeated changes reset its target counter. A mentor's growth benefit needs an under-23 mentee and an active same-club mentor. [Development guide](development.md).

### "My facility project is not happening immediately"

Direct academy/training investment in Finances is distinct from board-managed infrastructure projects. The latter use their own timelines, finances and decisions. There is no direct new-stadium purchase through the small investment action. [Club guide](club.md).

### "This match has no maps"

Games outside your club family can retain only result/scorer data. The game cannot show a full action report that was not retained. [Match guide](matches.md).

### "Continue failed, so did I lose the whole week?"

Completed days are kept. Inspect the reported date and error before retrying; another full-week advance means another week from the current date. [Time and saves](time-and-saves.md).

### "The handbook mentions a larger world than my career"

Older careers keep their world and calendars; deploying a new app version does not regenerate them. Historical expansion notes do not correspond to a control in the current player interface. Do not reseed or replace a career merely to make its world totals match a newly generated one. [Career compatibility](time-and-saves.md).

## What this handbook does not promise

The regulations are implemented models, not federation advice. Report ranges, potential, match estimates, player value and enterprise value are simulation judgments, not guarantees.

Current source behavior also does not prove that every older career has been migrated, every historical record reconstructed or every feature accepted on a live host. Check the actual career's status and in-game assessment whenever timing or eligibility matters.
