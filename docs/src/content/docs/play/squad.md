# Squad and player management

## Review the right group

**Squad** has independent team and age-group selectors. Select the first team or reserve team, then Senior, U19 or U17 as appropriate. Do not compare a reserve-team count with first-team requirements or assume an academy player has already been promoted.

Search, position and readiness filters help isolate a problem. Players remain grouped into goalkeeper, defender, midfielder and forward sections while the selected ordering applies within those sections.

Readiness has a specific meaning:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Injured | Injury return date is later than today |
| Low fitness | Not injured, fitness below 60 |
| Fit | Not injured, fitness at least 60 |

This is not a full match-eligibility certificate. A fit player can still be suspended, away on international duty or unregistered.

The squad table combines ability, potential, role, fitness, morale, appearances, goals, wage and availability. Use positional coverage to find shortages that an overall squad average would hide.

## Read the player dossier

Click a player's name to open six tabs:

| Tab | What to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Identity, headline ability/potential, current situation and performance |
| Career | Season lines, appearances, transfers, loans, honours and milestones |
| Attributes & development | Technical/physical/tactical/mental profile, personality, training and trajectory |
| Scouting | Reports, uncertainty, strengths, weaknesses and recruitment judgment |
| Contract & registration | Employer, wage, expiry, clauses and actual eligibility |
| Medical | Current injury, return estimate, wellbeing and recorded injury history |

Attributes are modeled on a 1-99 scale. Headline ability is a summary, not a substitute for position-specific attributes. Potential is dynamic, not an assurance that every prospect will reach a fixed ceiling.

Nationality, family heritage, languages, birthplace, international representation and employer location describe different things. Secondary nationalities can matter to recruitment and registration. Heritage percentages are narrative family roots, not ability modifiers or real-world demographic claims.

### Interpret history correctly

Permanent transfers and loans belong in transfer history. Movement between your own first team, reserve team and age cohorts is squad movement, not a sale or a new employer.

Season performance is grouped by season, club and competition. A transfer can create multiple lines in the same season; competition scorer totals can combine them. Friendly participation is not interchangeable with competitive season statistics.

Older saves may contain only backfilled current injuries and selected records, not a complete reconstructed career. Use dates and record labels when comparing players.

## Roles, captaincy and relationships

Squad roles express the player's place in the project, influence expectations and feed selection context. They are not a button that forces the manager to start him.

**Advice:** give a role you can plausibly support. Recruiting several players as key players into one position can create a squad that is strong on paper but impossible to satisfy.

Review language/social groups, relationship information and mentorship alongside ability. A captain or experienced mentor can serve a different purpose from a high-potential understudy. Do not assume a single morale score summarizes every relationship or wellbeing issue.

## Move a player, call him up or loan him?

| Need | Appropriate tool |
| --- | --- |
| Reassign an owned player to another eligible team/cohort in your club family | Permanent squad move |
| Cover a short-term shortage or give a temporary opportunity | Call-up |
| Ask coaches to look for higher-team minutes | Development request |
| Give a player a spell at a different club | Loan |
| Change the player's employer permanently | Transfer |

Internal moves keep ownership and wages intact. They are constrained by age cutoffs, destination squad capacity and loan status. A destination that is unavailable shows a reason: inspect that reason rather than looking for a transfer workaround.

Temporary call-ups can be for the next match, a number of days or until a date, and return automatically. They end at season rollover. A permanent move is not an active temporary call-up.

The coach can call up players automatically, or propose call-ups for you to accept when **Coach call-ups** is disabled in Settings. Read the proposal before the fixture date. See [development](development.md) for debut budgets and development requests.

## A practical depth audit

**Advice:** work position by position in the manager's actual formation:

1. Identify the first-choice player and a credible available replacement.
2. Check both players' fitness, contract expiry, registration and likely international absence.
3. Identify the next prospect, and decide whether his current team can provide suitable minutes.
4. Choose the smallest intervention that solves the gap: movement, call-up, training, loan or signing.

This avoids buying a player to solve a problem that was really an unprocessed registration, an injured starter or an academy pathway you had not reviewed.
