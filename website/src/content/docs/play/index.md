# Player handbook

Touchline puts you in charge of a football club's **sporting project**, not its touchline. You recruit players, negotiate contracts, shape development, support the manager and keep the club financially healthy. The manager selects the team and the simulation plays the matches.

Your first appointment begins on **1 July 2026**. Players, clubs, staff and results are fictional and procedurally generated. Familiar competition structures do not turn the world into a licensed real-life database.

## The core loop

1. **Review:** check Overview and Inbox for results, deadlines, injuries, offers and decisions.
2. **Diagnose:** inspect positional coverage, registration, contracts, finances and player reports.
3. **Act:** scout, negotiate, adjust development, make squad movements or respond to the board.
4. **Advance deliberately:** use Continue to simulate the next days, then inspect what changed.

Good results are not solely about buying the highest-rated player. A strong project combines available players in the manager's positions, affordable commitments, a route for prospects and enough resilience for injuries, international duty and failed deals.

## Rules versus advice

This handbook describes implemented behavior in the source snapshot used to build the documentation. **Rules** are enforced game constraints or explicit simulation behavior. **Advice** is a recommended way to work with those systems, not a guaranteed winning formula.

Touchline is a simulation with uncertainty: potential ranges, scout judgments, negotiations and match outcomes are not promises. Its football regulations are modeled rules, not a complete or authoritative reproduction of every association's regulations. An existing career may also have an older world or calendar than a newly generated career.

## Know what you do not control

There is no live lineup, substitution or minute-by-minute tactical control. The club's formation and style belong to the autonomous manager. You influence the available squad, roles, training, staff and set-piece preparation; call-ups and development requests create opportunities, not guaranteed starts.

The world continues around you. Other clubs trade, develop players, change managers, build facilities, form partnerships and change owners. A target's situation can change while you are planning a move.
