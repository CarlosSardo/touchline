# Registration and eligibility

## Four different questions

| Layer | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Signing | Can this contract or transfer legally and financially complete? |
| Association registration | Can this association register the player, including work-permission requirements? |
| Competition registration | Can this player be registered for this competition and season? |
| Match eligibility | Can he actually take part in this fixture today? |

**A signed player is not necessarily a playable player.** A transfer can complete while the player remains unregistered. GBE points, foreign quotas and squad lists do not themselves block signing; they are assessed afterwards.

Real signing constraints still apply: transfer timing, finances and embargoes, minor-transfer rules, valid contracts and loan rules. Separating the layers does not abolish those checks.

Read **Contract & registration** on the player dossier and the eligibility/status explanation. Labels combining **Signed** with **eligible** or **not registered**, or showing **Ineligible: insufficient GBE (9/15)**, describe different states, not interchangeable transfer failures.

## Windows and lists

The shared transfer-window model opens from **15 June through 1 September**, inclusive, and **1 January through the computed winter close**, inclusive. In the opening 2026/27 season the winter deadline is **1 February 2027**. The helper moves the close to 2 February in a year when 31 January is Saturday; use the in-game date/status instead of assuming every real-world association's calendar is reproduced.

Free-agent and emergency-loan routes have their own checks. Do not assume that any transfer can be completed outside a window merely because it was agreed earlier.

Competition registration is league/season-specific. Foreign quotas are evaluated here rather than at signing, and the player's recorded nationalities can matter. Full quotas, closed registration periods, embargoes or other list requirements can leave a newly employed player unavailable.

The simulation reviews registrations as days advance, including window openings and freed quota places. There is no universal instruction to manually submit every real-world squad list: follow the actual dossier assessment and available controls.

## England's GBE model

For players who need English work permission, Touchline uses its modeled **FA 2026/27** points rules. GB and Irish passport groups are exempt.

| Assessment | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 15 points or more | Points pass |
| 10-14 points | Exceptions Panel range; not automatically granted |
| Below the pass mark without an applicable pass/exemption | Do not assume registration will be granted |
| ESC assessment | Conditional route requiring eligibility and a free club place, not automatic permission |

The dossier's breakdown separates the criteria: international participation and ranking, domestic minutes and league standard, club performance and continental evidence. Missing data is labeled as no data, not silently treated as a measured zero.

Evidence changes as the career develops. Actual simulated minutes, results and caps feed reassessment; merely sending a player abroad for a year does not award automatic work-permit points.

**Important limitation:** the displayed Exceptions Panel and ESC assessments are not a player-controlled application flow that automatically grants registration. Do not budget on an assumed appeal win or an assumed available ESC place.

**Advice:** inspect the breakdown before offering wages. If an acquisition depends on future evidence, have a lawful, affordable playing pathway and a usable alternative for today's squad.

## International minors

Under-18 recruitment uses a simplified FIFA minor-transfer model. A nationality is not a universal passport to an international club move.

Same-association moves still face domestic youth rules. International transfers or first registrations generally need a modeled exception and, where required, an approved application. Routes include qualifying EU/EEA moves and specific family, border, residence or humanitarian circumstances; they depend on the player's recorded situation.

The EU/EEA route requires the relevant nationality and both associations within that area. It does not cover the UK simply because a player has an EU nationality.

Future transfers at 18 are distinct from immediate minor registration. Youth approaches may also involve amateur status, future professional contracts and training compensation. Use the assessment shown for the actual player rather than assuming every academy player is free to approach.

## Matchday availability adds more checks

Even a registered player may be injured, suspended, away with a national squad, too old for an age-limited competition or assigned to another team. The match selector also prevents a player from playing twice in one day.

Loan starts, returns and recalls change the playing-club context and trigger registration reassessment. Prior eligibility for the parent does not guarantee immediate eligibility at the borrower or on return.

## Diagnose an unavailable signing

1. Confirm the deal completed and the intended start date arrived.
2. Read association registration: permission, pending status or refusal reason.
3. Read competition registration: the right competition, season, quota and registration period.
4. Check injury, suspension, age cohort, international duty and current squad assignment.
5. Advance only when the stated issue needs game time; recruit another solution if there is no immediate lawful route.

Do not repeatedly renegotiate an already-completed transfer to fix a separate registration problem.
