# Matches, competitions and internationals

## Your role on matchday

Continue simulates fixtures. You do not select the starting eleven, make substitutions or issue live tactical instructions. The manager uses the formation, style, available players and supporting club context.

Selection excludes injured, suspended, internationally absent and otherwise ineligible players. Competitive club fixtures require registration; friendlies use different eligibility checks. Players cannot play twice on the same day. See [registration](registration.md) and [call-ups](development.md) before assuming every player in a club's ownership is available to every team.

The manager fills formation slots using primary positions, secondary positions and then alternatives. Fitness and role also affect selection priority; a promised squad role is not a manual lineup lock. Staff, chemistry, momentum and pressure contribute to the match context.

**Advice:** solve repeated selection problems by providing suitable, healthy, eligible depth. Do not buy a second expensive player in the same position while leaving a formation slot uncovered.

## Open the Match centre

Click a fixture or use **Match centre** in the sidebar. Select the age group and match. Scheduled fixtures show pre-match information; played matches show the detail that was retained.

Your club family uses the detailed engine. Other clubs' fixtures can be stored as **Result only**, with score and scorers rather than full pitch maps and individual reports. Missing analytics for such a game is a data-retention boundary, not a control you forgot to unlock.

### What each tab is for

| Tab | Use it to answer |
| --- | --- |
| Pitch analysis | Where did passes, carries, shots, defensive work and pressure happen? |
| Match statistics | Was the result supported by the overall performance? |
| Player performance | Which participants contributed, and how many minutes did they play? |
| Player report | What did one specific player's involvement look like? |
| Action log | Which recorded actions explain a pattern or incident? |
| Match story | How did the important events unfold? |

Pitch analysis offers pass, carry, dribble, shot, defensive, heat, pressing and fatigue maps. Filter by team, player and **Analysis up to minute**; pass maps also offer **Progressive only**. Team totals are match totals, so do not assume dragging the map time filter recalculates every statistic elsewhere on screen.

Player performance includes minutes, rating, goals, assists, passes, key passes, xG, xA, duels, tackles and saves. Click **Player report** to focus the individual analysis, or open the player dossier to compare performances over time.

## Interpret the numbers

**xG** is the model's expected-goal measure for shots, not the number of goals a team deserved with certainty. **xA** measures modeled chance creation. **xPts** estimates expected points from the model's scoring chances; it does not replace actual table points.

Possession, passing volume and a strong rating do not mean the same thing as chance quality or defensive security. A low-scoring defeat can coexist with a positive xG balance; a comfortable win can conceal weak chance creation.

**Advice:** compare a sequence of matches, then investigate individuals and positional coverage. Do not rebuild a squad around one noisy result. Use maps to ask narrower questions: was a striker receiving chances, was a flank repeatedly exposed, or did an apparently quiet midfielder move the ball forward?

The simulation includes substitutions, cards, injury exits, stoppage time and, where applicable, extra time and penalties. Suspensions are competition-scoped. Two-legged ties use the aggregate score; away goals are disabled. Penalty-shootout goals are separate from the match score.

## Read a competition properly

Open **Competitions** and filter by association, competition type and age group. Domestic leagues, continental competitions, cups, youth tournaments and internationals share the browser.

Competition pages expose standings or knockout information, fixtures/results, **Top scorers**, **Qualification** and **History**. Some competitions have conferences or split groups: select the relevant group before interpreting a displayed position.

Tables include actual points and supporting performance statistics. Financial sanctions or administration can deduct points; a gap between wins and points may be a sanction rather than a calculation mistake. Title, qualification and relegation zones depend on the modeled competition rules.

Use Qualification to inspect entry paths instead of assuming that every country or competition follows the same format. UEFA main phases and qualifying are modeled, as are continental structures elsewhere, but the game is not a complete real-world federation rulebook.

### Follow what matters

Favorite competitions and choose the available event alerts. This gives you a manageable watch list for first-team goals, youth development and recruitment rather than relying on a global stream of every event.

Top-scorer races support current and earlier seasons. Players level on goals share a rank; display ordering then uses fewer minutes, more assists and name. If a player represents multiple clubs in the same competition season, his goals are added together and the clubs are listed rather than split into rival entries.

## International football

**Internationals** provides Senior, U21, U20, U19 and U17 national-team views. National squads are selected by the simulation; your role remains club director, not national-team coach.

Players on international duty are unavailable to their clubs and can return with fitness or development consequences. Caps and participation appear in dossiers and can contribute evidence to the English work-permission model.

For youth events outside FIFA windows, review **Settings > Simulation > Release for youth tournaments outside FIFA windows** and the club release policy. Available modes are club policy, always and mandatory only. Do not assume every optional youth trip must be accepted, or that refusing an optional release bypasses mandatory obligations.

**Advice:** before a congested period, review likely international absences across the whole squad. A youth player who covers a senior shortage may himself be away at a tournament. Tie this review to [development planning](development.md) and [loan monitoring](loans.md).
