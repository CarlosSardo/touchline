# Loans and pathways

## Loans in and loans out start differently

To borrow a player, find him in the database, open the offer workflow and use an available loan route. Negotiate with the club and player under the borrowing rules.

To send your player out, open **Loans > Loan list** and make him available. **Other clubs initiate offers**. Review them under **Loan offers**, then accept, reject or counter. There is no general find-a-club destination search or lender-side loan-out negotiation path.

An affiliate-proposal dialog may contain a destination-club search; that search chooses a network partner, not an arbitrary loan-out destination.

**Advice:** list prospects before the moment you desperately need them placed. An offer depends on another club's need, level, finances and wage room; availability does not guarantee a suitable borrower.

## The nine loan types

| Type | Main purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Standard loan | Ordinary temporary spell |
| Short loan | Shorter, six-month-style cover |
| Development loan | A development-focused spell |
| Emergency loan | Exceptional cover under the emergency rules |
| Loan with option | Borrower can buy at an agreed fee |
| Loan with obligation | Purchase becomes compulsory under its conditions |
| Loan with sell-on | Preserve a future-sale interest |
| Two-year loan | Longer pathway where permitted |
| Cross-border loan | International spell subject to cross-border rules |

The name is not the whole agreement. Always read dates, wage split, promised position/role, minutes, recall and purchase clauses.

## Check the rules before the destination

Loans require a contracted player aged at least 16, valid ownership and a parent contract covering the spell. Free agents cannot be loaned. Normal windows and modeled FIFA international-loan limits apply.

Emergency loans outside windows are limited to the same association and the emergency conditions. A two-year label does not override the shorter limit on an international loan.

Signing the loan and registering the borrower are separate. A player can join contractually but still lack work permission or a quota place. Check [registration](registration.md) for both the borrower and eventual return.

## Negotiate a development environment

Incoming club offers set out a start date, end date, fee, borrower salary share, role, playing time, recall terms, purchase terms, sell-on, reason and reply-by date. Counter if the package does not suit the player's pathway.

The detailed terms can include a guaranteed position, training plan, mandatory minutes, development bonus, tactical-fit and manager-trust expectations, plus standard or injury recall.

**Advice:** compare the whole environment. A higher wage contribution is attractive financially, but not necessarily worth an unsuitable position, a crowded squad or weak prospects of minutes. A prestigious borrower is not automatically the best development destination.

Accepted deals start on their agreed date. Until then, do not count the player as having left or arrived.

## Monitor the spell

Use the **Loan tracker** rather than forgetting the player once he leaves. Its **Loan Success Score** is bounded from 0 to 100 and combines playing time, position use, tactical fit, development, performance, personality, lifestyle, injury, environment, trust and supporter context.

Read the weekly factor breakdown, cumulative minutes, score/pressure history and relationship context. A low score is a prompt to diagnose the cause, not proof that every borrower is failing for the same reason.

Loan experiences can affect ability progression, potential, morale, confidence, adaptability, value and relationships. Cross-border adaptation can improve language and adjustment; it does not bypass transfer law or guarantee GBE points.

**Advice:** review loans weekly. Compare actual minutes with the promise, check whether injuries explain a shortfall and use recall only when the agreement and timing permit it. The assistant can flag concerns, but does not automatically recall every disappointing loan.

## Endings, recalls and purchases

A recall must satisfy the agreed clause and applicable window rules. On ordinary expiry, the game resolves the purchase conditions or returns the player. A met obligation can be enforced even when the bill is uncomfortable; an option is not a guarantee of purchase.

On return, the parent's role/training context is restored and registration is reassessed. Retain wage room and a squad plan for returning players instead of treating every loan as a permanent departure.

Salary shares affect both budgets and weekly accounts. Purchase fees, development bonuses and sell-on rights create further commitments; look beyond the headline loan fee.

## Affiliates are a network, not an exemption

Affiliate agreements support feeder, friendly, development, scouting and foreign-adaptation purposes. Review relationship strength, costs and the actual agreement. Board requests can have review periods and cooldowns, so do not assume an affiliate is created immediately.

Some incoming permanent or future deals allow an onward **domestic affiliate loan**. It must end within the new contract, meet the affiliate conditions and cannot coexist with a seller loan-back.

An affiliate's embargo, administration, capacity or changed eligibility can invalidate a planned placement. The incoming signing need not fail merely because its optional onward loan can no longer proceed: check where the player actually ends up.

Future-signing affiliate placement and ordinary onward loans are not identical. A placed player's onward move has a modeled **112-day bridge period** and must occur inside a window. An affiliate is not a device to move an international minor immediately through a prohibited route.

**Advice:** plan a fallback at the parent club. Partnership strength and a nice-looking pathway are useful only if the player can legally register and realistically play.
